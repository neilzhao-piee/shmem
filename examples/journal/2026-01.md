## 2026-01-05 NTP drift on build1

After a suspend, build1's clock drifted ~40 min, which broke restic's "newer than last run" check and
skipped a backup. Fix applied: re-enabled NTP (`timedatectl set-ntp true`). Verified: `timedatectl`
shows synchronized, next nightly run picked up the missed files. Open: still investigating *why*
suspend disables NTP — suspected a systemd unit ordering issue on the VM template.

## 2026-01-08 ZFS pool nearly filled

restic's prune ran late and filled the `tank` pool to 99%, triggering ZFS auto-snapshot deletion.
Root cause: prune timer and backup timer could overlap when backup ran long. Fix: moved prune to
Sunday 03:30 (separate window) and added a 20%-free-space guard in `restic-nightly.service`.
Verified over two nightly cycles. Recorded in facts/fleet.md under "Known quirks".
